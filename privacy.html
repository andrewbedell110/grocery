GRUNDOW Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Information We Collect

GRUNDOW collects the following information to provide our meal planning service:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Household data: Household name and membership for shared meal planning.
  • Recipes: Recipes you create, save, or import, including ingredients, instructions, and photos.
  • Meal plans: Weekly meal plans and grocery lists you create.
  • Grocery store connection: If you connect your Smith's/Kroger account, we store OAuth tokens to enable cart integration. We do not store your grocery store password.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the meal planning experience.
  • To generate AI-powered recipe suggestions using your preferences.
  • To enable grocery list and Smith's/Kroger cart integration.
  • To allow sharing recipes with other users (only if you set your profile to public).

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS). All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Database and authentication provider.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Recipe suggestions are generated using the Claude AI API. Your recipe preferences are sent to generate suggestions but are not stored by Anthropic.
  • Kroger API: Used for product search and cart integration when you connect your Smith's/Kroger account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is only shared with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide our service.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Disconnect your Smith's/Kroger account.
  • Set your profile to private to prevent recipe sharing.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the app's settings page.
